Pilot Roadmap

Discovery to results in a structured 5-stage process

01

Discovery & Baseline Assessment

Map needs, stakeholders, capacity gaps, and quick-win use cases across selected schools and departments.

02

Co-design of Content & Use Cases

Co-create curriculum, delivery formats, and measurable outcomes aligned with Odisha AI Mission goals.

03

Workshops, Training & Departmental Pilots

Run practical demos, training sessions, and small pilots with real feedback loops.

04

Feedback Collection & Outcome Tracking

Collect adoption scores, satisfaction metrics, and replicability indicators at each site.

05

Pilot Report & Scale Recommendation

Deliver a comprehensive report with a district-level replication blueprint for statewide rollout.

Pilot success is not measured by event count — it is measured by adoption readiness, usability, and scalability.
